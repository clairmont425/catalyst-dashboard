What this is: a live look at the open positions of Polymarket’s most provably skilled traders — only the wallets that clear a strict, rules-based quality gate. Nobody is hand-picked or added because we “like” them; a wallet is in this list only because it passed every check below, and it drops off automatically the moment it stops passing.

How we choose the leaders — the gate (a wallet must pass ALL three):
① Top of the money leaderboard — we start only from the top 15 wallets on Polymarket’s OVERALL monthly profit leaderboard.
Why: these are real wallets putting real capital to work and winning at scale — not anonymous or random accounts. It anchors the pool to people the market itself ranks at the top.
② Win rate ≥ 55% — measured across their resolved (settled) positions.
Why: raw profit can come from a single giant lucky bet. A 55%+ hit rate is evidence of a repeatable edge — they are right more often than they are wrong, market after market — which is what makes a trader worth following rather than a one-time moonshot.
③ At least 20 resolved trades — that win rate must be drawn from 20+ markets that have actually settled.
Why: 3-for-3 is luck; 20+ closed positions make the win rate statistically meaningful, so a short hot streak can’t sneak a mediocre trader onto the list.
We compute each wallet’s win rate from its last ~1,000 resolved trades (wins ÷ settled), so the number reflects current skill, not ancient history. The gate is re-checked periodically; any wallet that slips below 55% (or whose track record thins out) is removed automatically.

How rows are ranked — composite score (0–100): within the qualified traders, each open position is scored by — trader win rate (0–30) + position size vs that trader’s own typical bet (0–25) + price room / upside left (0–15) + resolves within a tradeable window (0–15) + entry timing & P&L state (0–15). Higher = a stronger “proven trader, big conviction, room to run” setup.

Live & on demand: the data here is fetched straight from Polymarket in your own browser when you hit ⟳ Refresh live data — every position, price, and score is recomputed on your device. Nothing is precomputed or polled on a paid server, so it costs nothing to host and is always current the moment you ask for it.

What to copy: top-ranked rows are the cleanest “a proven trader bet bigger than usual on something with room to run.”
To find a trade: click the market title to open it on Polymarket.com directly, or hit 📋 to copy the title for pasting into the Polymarket US app. The “Polymarket US tradeable only” checkbox filters to markets with a confirmed .us match — uncheck it to see every position followed traders hold on .com.
Status — ACTIVE = fresh, normal-priced (best to copy); FAVORED / FADED = price near 1 / 0 but event NOT decided (e.g., NBA Finals heavy favorite — almost no upside left to copy); PARTIAL = trader has scaled out; RESOLVED / WON / LOST = market actually decided. Default filter shows only ACTIVE; uncheck to see everything.
vs Typical = position size as a multiple of the trader's median open-position size. 3x+ typical = real conviction.
